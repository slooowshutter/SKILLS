# Technical Breakthrough LinkedIn Posts

## Inputs

Gather or infer:

- The primary source and link
- The most interesting result and its mechanism
- Two or three defensible comparative metrics
- One limitation, reliability detail, or tradeoff
- Optional product and CTA for a separate P.S.

When claims may be current, verify them against primary sources. The fact sheet is complete when every number is supported and the mechanism can be explained in one plain-language sentence.

## Find the angle

Choose one central contrast:

- Old method versus new method
- Expected cause versus actual cause
- Complexity, size, or speed versus the achieved result

Build the whole post around that contrast. The angle is ready when the hook, explanation, evidence, and closing implication all express the same idea.

## Draft

Use this sequence:

1. **Hook:** State the result and hint at the unexpected mechanism.
2. **Reveal:** Name the mechanism in one short standalone line.
3. **Baseline:** Explain how the old approach works and where it struggles.
4. **Change:** Explain the new approach with concrete, visual language.
5. **Scale:** Add training size, scope, or implementation context when relevant.
6. **Proof:** Present two or three metrics as arrow bullets.
7. **Trust:** Mention a fallback, limitation, or reliability mechanism.
8. **Reframe:** Show what assumption the work challenges.
9. **Close:** End with a broader implication or genuine question.
10. **Link:** Add the primary source.
11. **P.S.:** If requested, separate the product CTA with `--`.

## Shape

```text
[Entity] achieved [specific result] by rethinking [unexpected mechanism].

[Short reveal.]

[How the conventional approach works and why it is costly.]

[How the new approach changes that.]

[Scale or scope evidence.]

What that produces:
→ [benchmark with comparison]
→ [benchmark with comparison]
→ [benchmark with comparison]

[Reliability detail or tradeoff.]

[Conceptual reframe.]

[Reflective implication or question.]

[Source link]

--
P.S. [Optional product benefit and direct CTA.]
```

## Voice

- Write with calm confidence and technical curiosity.
- Translate each specialist term immediately into ordinary language.
- Use specific nouns, active verbs, and numbers that prove the claim.
- Keep paragraphs to one to three sentences with generous blank lines; target roughly 180–320 words.
- Use arrow bullets for the benchmark section and at most one additional emoji.
- Keep the ending clean; make any P.S. commercially direct and structurally separate.

## Originality

Treat reference posts as structural evidence, not reusable prose. Generate fresh hooks, explanations, metaphors, sequencing, and closing questions. Preserve factual terminology only where technical accuracy requires it.

## Quality gate

Before returning the post, confirm:

- The first two lines create a clear information gap.
- The post teaches one central mechanism.
- Every metric has context or a comparison.
- The technical explanation is understandable without the source open.le 
- The reframe adds an idea rather than repeating the result.
- The CTA does not interrupt the educational argument.
- All factual claims remain faithful to the primary source.
